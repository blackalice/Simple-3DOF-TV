# Privacy policy

Simple 3DOF TV, version 1.0

## Short version

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it does not send anything to the author. Everything it remembers stays on your device. The only network connections are the ones you cause by using the web browser or (in the YouTube edition) YouTube.

## What is stored on your device

All of this is stored locally in the app's private storage, is not backed up to any cloud service, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data:

- Your settings (screen size, background, smoothing and so on).
- Where you stopped in each video (a file path or video id, its title, and a position) so you can resume.
- The path of the 360 degree photo you chose as a background, if any.
- YouTube edition only: your search history, watch history, and the list of channels you subscribed to or imported. Subscriptions are imported from a file you choose; the app never asks for your Google account.
- The built-in browser's cookies, cache and site data, like any browser. Clear them by clearing the app's data.

## What the app can see on your device

- Your videos and folders. The app asks for "all files access" (and media access) only so it can list and play the video files you choose and find subtitle files next to them. It does not upload, index to a server, or share them.
- The glasses over USB. The app opens only VITURE glasses and reads their head-tracking data.
- The device's motion sensors, for the air-mouse pointer. This data is used live and never stored.

## Network connections

- Browser: the pages you open (for example web search or Stremio) receive your IP address and the usual browser information. Their own privacy policies apply to what they collect.
- YouTube edition: searching, browsing and playing a video sends requests to YouTube and Google servers, which receive your IP address, a generic browser identifier, and what you ask for. The app does not sign in, and uses only the consent cookie needed to reach the pages. YouTube's and Google's privacy policies apply.
- Online video or subtitle addresses you open directly connect to those servers.

The app makes no other connections, and none to the author.

## VITURE's software

The app includes VITURE's head-tracking library. VITURE's library has an optional feature for reporting usage statistics to VITURE. This app does not turn it on. The library is compiled software that the author cannot inspect, so anything beyond that is governed by VITURE's own privacy practices. If you have questions about it, ask VITURE.

## Your choices

You can delete everything the app stores by clearing its data in Android settings, or by uninstalling it. Removing the "all files access" permission stops the app listing your folders; you can then still open files through Android's own picker where that is offered.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app and is shown in Settings, About and legal.
